Sample daily question

A stakeholder keeps requesting scope changes after sprint planning. What should the project manager do first?

Best answer

Review the change process and impact.

PMP questions often reward disciplined decision-making. First understand the request, assess impact, and follow the agreed process before approving or rejecting changes.
